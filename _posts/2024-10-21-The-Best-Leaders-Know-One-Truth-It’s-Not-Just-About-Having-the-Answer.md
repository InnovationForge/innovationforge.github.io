True leadership goes beyond technical expertise. Here are **7 Soft Skills Every Leader Needs** to build a great team and drive success:

1. **Emotional Intelligence**
    - Practice self-awareness and regularly check in with your team’s emotional state.

2. **Active Listening**
    - Listen to understand, not to respond. Focus fully on the speaker.

3. **Adaptability**
    - Embrace change and lead your team with a positive mindset when new situations arise.

4. **Conflict Resolution**
    - When conflicts arise, focus on the issue, not the person, to reach a resolution.

5. **Empathy**
    - Regularly ask your team how they’re doing and listen carefully to their responses.

6. **Persuasion**
    - Present your ideas with confidence and focus on how they benefit the entire team.

7. **Time Management**
    - Prioritize tasks and delegate effectively to ensure team members aren’t overwhelmed.
