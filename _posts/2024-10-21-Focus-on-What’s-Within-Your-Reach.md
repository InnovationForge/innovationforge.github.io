**Mindset Shift:** You don’t have to control everything to make progress. Instead, focus on what’s within your power. When you concentrate on what you can change, life feels much lighter. It's exhausting trying to manage what’s outside your control.

Here are **6 Ways to Focus on What You Can Control**:

1. **Master Your Mindset**
    - Choose how you interpret situations.
    - Remember, your thoughts shape your reality.

2. **Protect Your Energy**
    - Invest your time in what matters most.
    - Avoid wasting energy on draining tasks.

3. **Set Clear Boundaries**
    - Know when to say "no."
    - Guard your mental and emotional space.

4. **Be Intentional With Words**
    - Speak kindly to yourself and others.
    - Words influence your mood and actions.

5. **Reflect and Adjust**
    - Use failures as lessons, not setbacks.
    - Growth comes from reflection, not perfection.

6. **Control Your Actions**
    - Focus on effort, not results.
    - You control the input, not the outcome.

When you let go of what you can’t control, you give yourself the freedom to thrive in areas that truly matter. Embrace personal responsibility and focus on what brings real growth.
